Terms & Community Rules

Last updated: 1 November 2026

By using NIJØK you agree to these rules. They exist to keep travellers, riders and members safe.

Be kind and honest

  • No harassment, threats, hate, or sexual or violent content.
  • No illegal content, scams, spam or impersonation.
  • Post only photos and words you own or have the right to share.
  • Do not share other people’s private details (phone numbers, addresses, documents).

Travel posts

  • Alerts, routes and tips come from members, not from authorities. Check official sources before you travel. An alert older than 7 days may be out of date.
  • Meeting people for a trip or ride is your own decision. Meet in public places, tell someone your plans and use your judgement.
  • Ride and trip plans are not an offer of transport or a tour service.

Fitness and health

Fit & Move gives general targets only. It is not medical advice. If you have a health condition or an injury, talk to a doctor before changing your routine.

Reporting, blocking and moderation

Use Report on any post or profile and Block on any member. We may remove content, limit features or suspend accounts that break these rules.
