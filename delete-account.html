Delete your NIJØK account

In the app (fastest):
  1. Open NIJØK and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account & Security → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE, enter your password and confirm.

Cannot open the app?

Email elixir.shivadas@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my NIJØK account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username and photo.
  • Your posts, stories, travel posts and traveller profile.
  • Your notes, follows, blocks and Fit & Move data.
  • Your sign-in.

What may remain

  • Replies you wrote on other members’ posts.
  • Encrypted messages already delivered to other members of a chat (we cannot read them).
  • Shop enquiries you sent, which we may keep for a short time for record keeping.

Questions? Write to elixir.shivadas@gmail.com.
