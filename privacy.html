NIJØK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

NIJØK ("we", "the app") is a chat, groups, notes, community and food-knowledge app. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. The app is operated by Boske Shivadas, India. Contact: elixir.shivadas@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number or email address, and a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password).
  • Profile: name, username, "about" text, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Content you create: chat messages, photos, voice messages, notes, community posts, comments and likes, groups you create or join.
  • Contacts you save inside the app and groups you are a member of.
  • Technical data: basic device and connection information that Google Firebase uses to run the service.

Your scan history is stored only on your own device and is not uploaded.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and show your profile to people you chat with.
  • To deliver messages, host groups, show community posts and connect voice calls.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information and the app does not show third-party ads.

3. Who can see what

  • Your mobile number is not shown to other users in the app. Others find you by your username.
  • Your name, username, about text and profile photo are visible to other users of the app.
  • Community posts, comments and likes are visible to other signed-in users.
  • Chat messages are encrypted on your device before they are stored. Community posts, public groups and channels are not private.
  • Voice calls connect your devices directly (peer to peer). Calls are not recorded by us.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf under Google's own security and privacy terms. Voice calls use public STUN servers to connect devices.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, username, notes, posts and comments. Messages you sent in other people's chats may remain visible to those people unless they delete them.

6. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, delete your own messages and posts, block or leave chats and groups, and ask us to delete your account and data. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may also ask to access or correct your data and to withdraw your consent. Write to elixir.shivadas@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

7. Children

NIJØK is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Safety and reporting

Do not post illegal, abusive or harmful content. We may remove content or accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, write to elixir.shivadas@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
