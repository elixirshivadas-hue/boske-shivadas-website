    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 3 November 2026. This policy explains what NIJOK collects, why, and the choices you have.

    About NIJOK and this policy

    NIJOK is an app for chat, community (Openworld), notes, travel (Wandrø), fitness (Fit & Move), food and book information, and a small shop. It is run by Boske Shivadas, Hyderabad, India (“we”, “us”). NIJOK is meant for people aged 18 and over.

    This policy applies to the NIJOK app, the website at nijok.in and these policy pages. By creating an account you confirm that you have read it.

    What we collect

    • Account: your email address, name, optional username, profile photo, cover photo and about text.
    • Things you create: posts, comments, stories (visible for 24 hours), travel posts (stories, ride routes, alerts, questions, travel-buddy plans), your traveller profile (role, bio, city, optional Instagram or YouTube link), notes, photos you choose to add, and shop enquiries (your name, email and the items you ask about).
    • Chats: messages are end-to-end encrypted. We store the encrypted messages and the public keys needed to deliver them. We cannot read them.
    • Location: only when you tap “Pin my location” on a travel post (saved as an approximate point, about 1 km) or the compass button on the map (used on your device to move the map, not saved). If you do not pin a location, a post uses the place you chose.
    • Fit & Move: workouts, steps, water, sleep, goals and body stats that you enter. They are kept on your device and synced to your account so you can restore them on a new phone.
    • Technical data: our service providers (Google Firebase, Google Maps) process technical data such as IP address and device details when the app connects to them.
    • On this website: your light/dark choice and cookie choices are saved in your browser only (see Cookie & Storage Policy).

    How we use it

    To run your account, show your content to the people you share it with, deliver chats, show the map, answer shop enquiries, keep the community safe (reports, blocks, moderation) and fix problems. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

    Who else handles data

    ServiceWhy we use it
    Google FirebaseSign-in, database and file storage.
    Google MapsMap display in Wandrø and Explore.
    Public information services (for example Wikipedia, weather, Open Library, Open Food Facts)Fill Explore, Books and Food pages. They do not receive your account details.

    We do not give your personal data to advertisers or data brokers. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect people from serious harm.

    What other people can see

    Posts, stories, comments, travel posts and your traveller profile are visible to other signed-in members. Do not put private details such as phone numbers or home addresses in public posts.

    Keeping and deleting data

    You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Account & Security → Delete account, or right here on the Delete Account page. Deleting removes your profile, posts, stories, travel posts and traveller profile, notes, follows, blocks and Fit & Move data.

    Replies you wrote on other people’s posts, and encrypted messages already delivered to other members of a chat, may remain for those members. Our service providers may keep limited technical logs for a short time under their own terms.

    Security

    Data is sent over HTTPS. Chats are end-to-end encrypted. Access to our database is limited by security rules, and your password is handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

    Your choices and rights

    You can edit or delete your content, block or report other members, stop sharing location (we only use it when you tap the button), and delete your account at any time. You can also write to us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion, and to withdraw consent.

    If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal. Use the contact details below and we will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

    Children

    The app is not for people under 18. If you think a child has joined, write to us and we will remove the account.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.

    Community Guidelines

    Last updated: 3 November 2026. NIJOK is a place to learn, connect, explore and grow. These rules keep it that way.

    Be kind and respectful

    • No harassment, bullying, hate or threats against any person or group.
    • Disagree politely. Discuss ideas, not people.

    Be honest

    • Do not impersonate others, run scams or post fake reviews, fake alerts or false news.
    • In Wandrø, share what you really saw or did. Say when something may be out of date, and mark tips that are still correct.

    • Do not post anyone’s private details (phone number, home address, ID numbers, photos taken without consent).
    • No violence, self-harm encouragement, dangerous challenges or illegal activity.

    Only post photos, text and videos you own or have permission to use. Do not copy other people’s work.

    No spam

    No repeated posts, unwanted promotions, mass messages or fake accounts.

    Use Report on a post, comment or profile, or Block a member, from the app. You can also write to us at elixir.shivadas@gmail.com.
