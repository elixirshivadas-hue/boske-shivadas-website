Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

NIJØK is an app for chat, community, notes, travel (Wandrø), fitness (Fit & Move) and a small shop. It is run by Boske Shivadas, India (“we”, “us”). This page explains what information the app uses and why. NIJØK is meant for people aged 18 and over.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, name, optional username, profile photo and about text.
  • Things you create: posts, comments, stories (visible for 24 hours), travel posts (stories, ride routes, alerts, questions, travel-buddy plans), traveller profile (role, bio, city, optional Instagram or YouTube link), notes, photos you choose to add, and shop enquiries (your name, email and the items you ask about).
  • Chats: messages are end-to-end encrypted. We store the encrypted messages and the public keys needed to deliver them. We cannot read them.
  • Location: only when you tap “Pin my location” on a travel post (saved as an approximate point, about 1 km) or the compass button on the map (used on your device to move the map, not saved). If you do not pin a location, a post uses the place you chose.
  • Fit & Move: workouts, steps, water, sleep, goals and body stats that you enter. They are kept on your device and synced to your account so you can restore them on a new phone.
  • Technical data: our service providers (Google Firebase, Google Maps) process technical data such as IP address and device details when the app connects to them.

How we use it

To run your account, show your content to the people you share it with, deliver chats, show the map, answer shop enquiries, keep the community safe (reports, blocks, moderation) and fix problems. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

Who else handles data

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and database) and Google Maps (map display).
  • Public information services (for example Wikipedia or weather data) are used to fill Explore pages. They do not receive your account details.

What other people can see

Posts, stories, comments, travel posts and your traveller profile are visible to other signed-in members. Do not put private details such as phone numbers or home addresses in public posts.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Account & Security → Delete account. This removes your profile, posts, stories, travel posts and profile, notes, follows, blocks and Fit & Move data. Replies you wrote on other people’s posts, and encrypted messages already delivered to other members of a chat, may remain for those members. You can also ask us to delete your data from the account deletion page.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your content, block or report other members, stop sharing location (we only use it when you tap the button) and ask us for a copy of, or correction to, your data by email.

Children

The app is not for people under 18. If you think a child has joined, write to us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
